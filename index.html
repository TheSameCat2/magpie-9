    FLIGHT

    Gravity pulls. Idle and the drone drops. Flap climbs — each flap is a pulse, not a hover. Strafe sideways to thread the hatch. Walls, plates, and beams end the run.

    DAMPER

    The gold orb trims your scroll speed and the trim sticks for the rest of the run. Every gate you clear pushes the conduit faster, so a damper buys you back some reaction time. Steer through as many as you can.

    SPARE LIFE

    The green orb adds a spare, shown as a + at the top right. Crash with a spare and the run rewinds to just inside the last gate you cleared, then waits for your tap. In a real run one spare appears somewhere in every ten-gate sector.

    SHUNT

    The blue orb engages the shunt overdrive: the next 5 gates count double while the conduit runs faster. A hit burns the whole charge, so spend it cleanly.
